      # Hardware Accessories

      The finishing details that help your doors and spaces work as they should. Edina supplies and installs hardware accessories selected to suit your project and complement your handles and locks.
  - type: markdown
    content: |
      - **Hinges:** selected to suit the door’s size, weight and application, providing support and smooth operation.
      - **Door Stops:** wall or floor-mounted options that limit door travel and help protect walls, handles and surrounding surfaces.
      - **Roller Latches:** hold a door closed without a lever-operated latch, commonly paired with pull handles and a separate lock where required.
      - **Barrel Bolts:** provide additional fastening for suitable doors, including securing the inactive leaf of a double door set.
      - **Robe Hooks:** practical hanging points for robes, towels and clothing, with finishes to coordinate with your other hardware.
