      # The Edina Process

      We aim to make your project straightforward and transparent from start to finish. From the first quote through to installation and handover, we keep you informed about what’s included, what happens next and when payments are due.
  - type: features
    intro_content: |
      ## Nine steps from quote to aftercare
    items:
      - icon: hugeicons:note-edit
        name: 01 — Initial quote
        description: Tell us what you have in mind. Photos, approximate measurements and your preferred door styles help us prepare an initial quote, subject to site measurements and final selections.
      - icon: hugeicons:ruler
        name: 02 — Site measure
        description: We visit your property to confirm measurements, assess existing frames and openings, and discuss any installation requirements.
      - icon: hugeicons:checkmark-circle-02
        name: 03 — Scope confirmation
        description: We confirm your doors, frames, glazing, hardware and finishing details, along with inclusions, exclusions and the final price. Any required contract documents are provided before proceeding.
      - icon: hugeicons:credit-card
        name: 04 — Deposit
        description: Once the scope and paperwork are accepted, we issue the deposit request in line with the agreed payment terms and applicable requirements.
      - icon: hugeicons:cube
        name: 05 — Materials procurement
        description: We order the approved doors, frames and hardware. Manufacturing and delivery lead times vary, and we keep you updated on expected availability.
      - icon: hugeicons:calendar-03
        name: 06 — Installation scheduling
        description: As materials become available, we arrange a suitable installation date and explain any preparations needed, including access and clearing the work area.
      - icon: hugeicons:house-01
        name: 07 — Installation
        description: We carry out the agreed work, fit and adjust the hardware, and check that the doors operate correctly. Any changes to the confirmed scope are discussed and agreed before proceeding.
      - icon: hugeicons:checkmark-circle-02
        name: 08 — Completion & payment
        description: We walk through the completed work with you, explain operation and care, and address any outstanding items. The completion balance is then due under the agreed payment terms.
      - icon: hugeicons:shield-01
        name: 09 — Warranty & aftercare
        description: Your installation is backed by Edina’s workmanship warranty and the applicable manufacturers’ product warranties. Warranty details and the installation defects period are confirmed in your agreement and provided at handover, alongside care instructions. If an issue arises, contact us so we can assess it and arrange any covered rectification.
