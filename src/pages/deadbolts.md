      # Deadbolts

      Our selected Handle House deadbolts provide a straightforward locking option for entrance doors, paired with your choice of lever or pull handle.

      With an adjustable 60 mm or 70 mm backset, they can suit a range of new and existing door preparations. Backset is the distance from the edge of the door to the centre of the lock cylinder.
  - type: markdown
    content: |
      ## Locking options

      - **Double Key:** a key is required to lock and unlock the deadbolt from both inside and outside.
      - **Key & Thumbturn:** key operation on the outside, with a thumbturn on the inside for locking and unlocking without a key.
