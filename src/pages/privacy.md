      # Privacy policy

      We use the details you send us to respond to your enquiry and discuss your job.

      Our contact forms use [Formspark](https://formspark.io/), which processes and stores submitted details. Email enquiries are handled through our email provider.

      Our website host may record technical information such as your IP address, requested pages and request times. Site search runs in your browser; search terms may also appear in page URLs and hosting logs.

      Please keep lock codes, passwords, payment details and other sensitive information out of your enquiry.

      For questions about your information, email [info@edinadoors.com.au](mailto:info@edinadoors.com.au).
