      ## Frames, hardware and the site measure
    items:
      - question: Which door ranges can use a suitable existing frame?
        answer: "Edina’s [timber entry doors](/timber-entry-doors/) and [internal doors](/internal-doors/) can use suitable existing frames. Both can also be supplied with new frames and hardware. A style code alone does not confirm whether a door will suit your frame."
      - question: Can an aluminium entrance door be put into my old frame?
        answer: "No. **Aluminium door retrofits:** aluminium entrance doors are supplied only as complete door-and-frame packages. See the [aluminium range](/aluminium-entry-doors/)."
      - question: Can the existing locks and handles stay?
        answer: "**Reusing existing locks and hardware:** our installations use new hardware selected to suit the door and application. Hardware selection belongs in the quote, even when a frame is retained."
      - question: Will an old security screen be refitted?
        answer: "**Refitting existing security screens:** in most cases, existing security screen doors are unlikely to be compatible with the new door frame. We don’t refit existing screens, so any replacement or alterations will need to be arranged separately."
      - question: When is the decision checked?
        answer: "During the [site measure](/process/), Edina confirms measurements, assesses existing frames and openings, and discusses installation requirements. The scope then confirms doors, frames, glazing, hardware, finishing details, inclusions, exclusions and final price."

      ## Put these details in your enquiry

      Send photos of the door and opening, approximate height and width, your preferred style if you have one, and the suburb. Say whether you want to retain the frame, rather than treating that as an agreed part of the job.

      If a security screen or surrounding finish must remain, mention it early. The [complete exclusions](/what-we-do/) also cover structural alterations, brickwork, rendering and tiling, so a door replacement is not an agreement to change the building opening or make good every surrounding surface.
