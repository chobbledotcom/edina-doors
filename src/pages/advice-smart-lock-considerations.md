      # What should you ask before choosing a smart lock?

      **Editorial draft — Michael’s practical guidance is still pending.** The model facts below come from the existing Edina range information. Confirm current specifications and suitability for your door before choosing a product.

      A smart-lock enquiry has two parts: will it suit the door, and will its everyday operation suit the people using it?

      ## Separate the decisions
    items:
      - question: Can Edina fit a lock I buy elsewhere?
        answer: "Edina exclusively supplies and installs Vault smart locks. **Installation of products supplied by others:** we supply the doors, hardware and materials we install. See the [full service scope](/what-we-do/)."
      - question: Can an existing door take a smart lock?
        answer: "Vault locks can be supplied for compatible existing doors, subject to assessment of the door, frame and hardware. This is not universal compatibility. The selected Vault Prime information also states that it is not suitable for fire-rated doors. See the [current selected range](/smart-locks/)."
      - question: Which access methods matter to the household?
        answer: "The selected range includes different combinations of fingerprint, PIN, RFID, phone/app, physical key and facial recognition. Check the specific model rather than assuming every lock offers every method. Tell Michael which methods you actually want to use."
      - question: Does remote access need extra equipment?
        answer: "The selected Nexus, Prime and Guardian information lists an optional Vault G5 Gateway for remote functions. Fortress Vision has integrated Wi-Fi and does not require a separate G5 Gateway. Requested functions and the selected model must be confirmed together."
      - question: What happens when the battery needs attention?
        answer: "Power arrangements differ: Prime uses four AA batteries, not included; Nexus and Fortress Vision use rechargeable lithium batteries. The range information lists physical-key backup access on these models, but this draft does not provide a universal flat-battery procedure. Ask for the exact model’s power, warning and backup instructions at handover."
      - question: What is included in installation?
        answer: "Edina’s service description includes fitting, setup and a demonstration of the lock’s features and everyday operation. **Electrical and security wiring:** hardwired access control, alarms or electrical connections remain outside the stated scope."

      ## Bring the use case, not your access codes

      Tell Michael whether the entrance is used by family, guests or trades; whether remote functions are needed; and what door and hardware you already have. Send useful door photographs, but do not send PINs, passwords or private app screenshots through a general enquiry.

      Fortress Vision’s existing range information recommends strong, stable Wi-Fi at the door and notes that charging is generally required every 20–60 days, depending on usage and Wi-Fi conditions. That is model-specific information, not a battery promise for the entire range.

      - What door details and photographs are needed for a compatibility check?

      - What app-account and access-management guidance belongs in handover?
      - Which questions should go to the manufacturer rather than be guessed from a product photograph?
