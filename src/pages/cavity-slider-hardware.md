      ## Hardware options

      - **Passage Sets:** flush pulls for doors that don’t require locking, such as laundries, wardrobes and living areas.
      - **Privacy Sets:** an internal thumbturn provides privacy for bathrooms and bedrooms, with an external emergency release.
      - **Edge Pulls:** allow the door to be pulled out when fully recessed into the wall pocket.
