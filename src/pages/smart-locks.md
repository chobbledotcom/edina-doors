      ## Vault Prime

      Supplied and installed by Edina Doors.

      The Prime has a slimline lever with a fingerprint reader built into the handle, plus PIN, card and phone access.

      Available in black, silver and gold.

      **Key features**

      - Fingerprint, PIN code, RFID card and Bluetooth/app access.
      - Physical key for backup access.
      - Permanent or temporary access for family, guests and trades.
      - Access history available through the Vault app.
      - Optional Vault G5 Gateway enables remote access, notifications and Google Home or Alexa integration.

      **Specifications**

      - Dimensions: 60 mm wide × 240 mm high × 75 mm deep.
      - Recommended clearance: 80 mm.
      - Power: four AA batteries, not included.
      - External weather rating: IP55.
      - Tubular latch or mortice configuration to suit the application.

      Suitable for compatible new doors and existing door upgrades, subject to assessment. Not suitable for fire-rated doors.
