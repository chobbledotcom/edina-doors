      ## Sizes, frames and finishing

      Available in standard door sizes and supplied unfinished in raw timber, ready for sealing and finishing. Selected models are also available in 1200 mm widths.

      All timber doors can be fitted into suitable existing frames or supplied and installed as a complete door package, including the frame, hardware and glazing where required. Choose from a range of glass options, hardware finishes and [door frame configurations](/entry-door-frame-configurations/) to suit your home.

      Edina does not paint, stain or seal doors and timber components; those require finishing by others. New doors can be supplied with [Handle House hardware](/door-hardware/) selected to suit the door and application.
