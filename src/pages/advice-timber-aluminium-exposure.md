      ## Compare the package, not just the surface

      Edina’s selected solid timber range is manufactured by Door City. Timber doors are supplied unfinished in raw timber, ready for sealing and finishing, and may use a suitable existing frame or a complete new package.

      The selected Parkwood aluminium entrance range is available in custom sizes, subject to the design, and six powder-coat colours. It is supplied only as a complete new entry set, including a new frame.

      Neither description, on its own, determines the correct choice for an exposed entrance.

      ## The confirmed differences

      | Question | Timber entry range | Aluminium entry range |
      | --- | --- | --- |
      | Can a suitable existing frame remain? | Yes, subject to assessment. | No: a complete new door-and-frame set is required. |
      | How is the surface supplied? | Unfinished raw timber, ready for sealing and finishing. | A selected powder-coat finish, with availability confirmed when quoting. |
      | Who handles timber finishing? | Finishing by others; Edina does not paint, stain or seal. | Do not assume the package includes finishing surrounding timber components. |
      | What sizes and configurations apply? | Standard sizes, with selected models in 1200 mm widths; final selections are confirmed. | Custom sizes subject to the selected design; dimensions confirmed when quoting. |

      ## Exposure questions that need a real answer
    items:
      - question: Does the entrance receive direct sun, rain or salt exposure?
        answer: Tell Michael what you observe and provide photos showing the entrance and its shelter. This draft does not rank materials for western sun, wind-driven rain or salt air; the selected product and manufacturer requirements need checking.
      - question: What finishing and maintenance does the chosen door require?
        answer: "Ask for the selected manufacturer’s instructions and clarify who arranges the work. Edina’s scope is explicit: **Painting, staining or sealing:** doors and timber components require finishing by others. No maintenance interval has been invented here."
      - question: What warranty will apply to my package?
        answer: "Your installation is backed by Edina’s workmanship warranty and the applicable manufacturers’ product warranties. Warranty details and the installation defects period are confirmed in your agreement and provided at handover, alongside care instructions. If an issue arises, contact us so we can assess it and arrange any covered rectification."
