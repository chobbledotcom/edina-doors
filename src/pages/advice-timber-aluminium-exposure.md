      # Timber or aluminium: what does your entrance need?

      Timber doors can use a suitable existing frame and require sealing and finishing by others. Aluminium doors come as a complete new entry set with a new frame. Exposure and the selected manufacturer’s requirements also need checking before you choose.

      ## Exposure, care and warranty
    items:
      - question: Does the entrance receive direct sun, rain or salt exposure?
        answer: Tell Michael what you observe and send photos showing the entrance and its shelter. Include western sun, wind-driven rain or salt air in that discussion. The selected product’s requirements need checking for the site.
      - question: What finishing and maintenance does the chosen door require?
        answer: "Ask for the selected manufacturer’s instructions and clarify who arranges the work. **Painting, staining or sealing:** doors and timber components require finishing by others."
      - question: What warranty will apply to my package?
        answer: "Your installation is backed by Edina’s workmanship warranty and the applicable manufacturers’ product warranties. Warranty details and the installation defects period are confirmed in your agreement and provided at handover, alongside care instructions. If an issue arises, contact us so we can assess it and arrange any covered rectification."
