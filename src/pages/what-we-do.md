      # What We Do

      Edina Doors provides door supply and installation, alongside finishing carpentry, for homeowners and builders across the Sunshine Coast. From individual replacements to complete fit-outs, our work includes:
  - type: markdown
    content: |
      ## Edina Does

      ### Door supply & installation

      Complete packages bringing together doors, frames, glass and hardware to suit your home or building project. Explore the [door ranges](/doors/).

      ### Internal doors

      Replacement doors and new installations, including flush, shaker and barn door styles. Options include individual rooms, whole-home upgrades and new builds. See [internal doors](/internal-doors/).

      ### Entrance & external doors

      Timber and aluminium entrance packages, including hinged and pivot doors, double doors, sidelights and transoms. Frames, glazing and hardware can be selected to suit the opening. See [timber entry doors](/timber-entry-doors/), [aluminium entry doors](/aluminium-entry-doors/) and [frame configurations](/entry-door-frame-configurations/).

      ### Retrofitting into existing frames

      New doors fitted to suitable existing frames, with new hinges, handles and locks. A practical option for updating doors without replacing the entire door set. This applies to our [timber](/timber-entry-doors/) and [internal](/internal-doors/) ranges, not to aluminium entrance doors.

      ### Custom door & frame solutions

      Door and frame combinations for openings that need something beyond a standard replacement, including custom sizes, configurations, sidelights and overhead glazing. Start with the [frame configurations](/entry-door-frame-configurations/).

      ### Smart lock supply & installation

      We exclusively supply and install Vault smart locks, with options for new door packages or retrofitting to compatible existing doors. Installation includes fitting, setup and a demonstration of the lock’s features and everyday operation. See [smart locks](/smart-locks/).

      ### Finishing carpentry

      Supply and installation of skirting, architraves, mouldings and trims, along with window reveals, sills and wall panelling. Available for individual upgrades or as part of a broader interior fit-out.

      ### Homeowner packages

      Supply and installation packages for renovations, replacements and new entrances. We can include removal of existing doors, new frames, hardware and surrounding trims to suit the work required. Read [how a job works](/process/).

      ### Builder fix-out packages

      Door supply, installation and finishing carpentry for residential building projects, including internal doors, jambs, hardware, skirting and architraves. Packages can cover a defined stage of work or the complete carpentry fix-out.
  - type: callout

    name: Edina Doesn’t
    content: |
      To keep the scope clear from the outset, our services do not include:

      - **Painting, staining or sealing:** doors and timber components require finishing by others.
      - **Installation of products supplied by others:** we supply the doors, hardware and materials we install.
      - **Refitting existing security screens:** in most cases, existing security screen doors are unlikely to be compatible with the new door frame. We don’t refit existing screens, so any replacement or alterations will need to be arranged separately.
      - **Aluminium window supply or installation:** our aluminium range is focused on entrance door packages.
      - **Reusing existing locks and hardware:** our installations use new hardware selected to suit the door and application.
      - **Structural alterations:** enlarging openings, changing lintels or modifying load-bearing walls.
      - **Brickwork, rendering and tiling:** making good surrounding masonry or tiled surfaces.
      - **Electrical and security wiring:** hardwired access control, alarms or electrical connections.
      - **Aluminium door retrofits:** aluminium entrance doors are supplied only as complete door-and-frame packages.
      - **Standalone repairs:** general lock repairs, emergency locksmith work or repairs to doors outside an Edina installation.
